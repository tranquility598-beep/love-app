# Love v2.0.0 — «Communication, reimagined.» 🖤

> RU ниже ⬇️

## ✨ What's new

**Love 2.0 is a full reforging.** Calmer, faster, and finally alive on every screen.

- 🎙️ **Voice Rooms 2.0** — a brand-new voice experience with *presence orbs*, buttery screen-share up to **1080p @ 60fps**, and webcam support. No more loopback lag — the engine was rebuilt from the ground up.
- 🖥️ **Screen share, perfected** — pick any window or display, crisp text (no more blur), instant start, clean stop.
- 💬 **Realtime everything** — messages, calls and presence land the moment they happen. No refresh, ever.
- 🎨 **Wabi-Sabi design** — a quiet black & white world built for focus. Profiles, moods, music and hobbies, all in one calm shell.
- 🎵 **Profile music & moods** — show what you're listening to and how you feel.
- 📱 **Native Android build** — Love now lives in your pocket. Same app, same account, anywhere.
- 🛠️ **Hundreds of fixes** — mic/mute reliability, notifications you can dismiss, friends, profiles, and a lot of polish.
