Love is currently in early access. You can request an invitation on our main page — after review, you'll receive an access code via email. Access is granted manually while the platform is being tested.

You can report bugs directly inside the app: open Love Hub → "Report a Bug", or use the form below on this page. Please include steps to reproduce and your app version.

First, try resetting your password via the login screen. If you no longer have access to your email, contact us using the form below with your username and registration email — we'll help verify your identity.

Servers are traditional community spaces with channels, categories, and roles. Rooms are a simpler format — a single space for text and voice, ideal for small groups and focused conversations.

Make sure Love has microphone permissions in your system settings (Windows: Settings → Privacy → Microphone). Also check that the correct input device is selected in Love Settings → Audio.

Love updates automatically. When a new version is available, you'll see a banner at the top of the app — click it to download and restart. You can always check your current version in Settings → About.
