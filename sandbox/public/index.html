Community Rules

A calm, respectful space

Love is a cozy place. A few simple rules keep it good for everyone.

01

Respect

No insults, harassment or discrimination. Treat others the way you'd like to be treated.

02

No spam

Don't flood chats with ads, spam or repeated messages.

03

Safety

Never share others' personal data or impersonate other people.

04

Appropriate content

Illegal and offensive content is forbidden. Be considerate.

05

Help out

Spotted a violation? Report it via Support — we'll take a look.

Breaking the rules may lead to limited access to the app.

Philosophy

Built for focus.

Love is designed to feel calm, responsive, and intentional.

Every interface decision prioritizes clarity, communication, and usability over noise and distraction. No algorithms, no ads, no trackers. Just you and your connections.
